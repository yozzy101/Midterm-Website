Join Us for the 73rd Annual Ox Roast!

Hosted by the Leroy Township Firefighter's Association

For 73 years strong, the Leroy Township Firefighter's Association has been proud to host this wonderful community event. We thank you for your continuous support, which has made a significant impact on our department and community!
